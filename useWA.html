WhatsApp Guide

Learn everything about WhatsApp - from messaging to privacy settings. Perfect for beginners and first-time users.

Messaging

Send text messages, voice notes, photos, documents, and videos. Always verify unknown numbers before replying to avoid scams.

  • Text messages with emojis and stickers
  • Voice messages (hold mic button to record)
  • Share photos, videos, and documents
  • React to messages with emojis
  • Reply to specific messages
Chat Screen
WhatsApp chat interface

Voice & Video Calls

Make free calls over internet. Avoid answering unknown international numbers and be cautious of call scams.

  • Voice calls to any contact worldwide
  • Video calls with up to 8 people
  • Switch between voice and video
  • Mute, speaker, and end call controls
  • Never answer unknown international numbers
Video Call
Voice and video calling features

Groups

Connect with multiple people in groups. Only admins can add members. Stay safe in public groups.

  • Create groups with up to 1024 members
  • Group admins control who can add members
  • Mute notifications for quiet groups
  • Send broadcast messages to multiple contacts
  • Avoid sharing personal info in public groups
Group Chat
Group chat management

Status Updates

Share photos, videos, and text that disappear after 24 hours. Control who can see your status.

  • Post photos and videos as status
  • Text status updates with backgrounds
  • Choose who can view your status
  • See who viewed your status
  • Reply privately to status updates

Privacy & Security Settings

Last Seen & Online

Control who can see your last seen status - everyone, contacts, or nobody.

Profile Photo Privacy

Choose who can see your profile picture - everyone, contacts, or nobody.

About Information

Control who can see your "About" section and status information.

Read Receipts

Turn off blue ticks to hide when you've read messages (except in groups).

Two-Step Verification

Add extra security with a PIN required to register your number again.

Block Contacts

Block spam numbers and unwanted contacts to stop messages and calls.

Quick WhatsApp Tips

• Pin important chats to the top for easy access
• Star important messages to find them later
• Use search to find messages, photos, or links
• Backup your chats to Google Drive to save history
• Enable fingerprint lock for extra security
• Use WhatsApp Web on your computer

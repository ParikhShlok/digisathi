Critical Safety Knowledge

Secure vs Unsecure Websites

Learn to identify safe websites and protect yourself from online threats. Understanding website security is essential for safe internet browsing.

Secure Websites

Websites with HTTPS encryption and proper security certificates. They protect your data and are safe for online transactions.

Safe & Protected

Unsecure Websites

Websites without proper security measures. They can expose your personal and financial information to hackers and cybercriminals.

What is Hacking?
Hacking is the unauthorized access to computer systems to steal, manipulate, or destroy data for malicious purposes.

⚠️ Critical Safety Tip

Always check for HTTPS and the lock icon before entering any personal information on a website. Never enter sensitive data like passwords, bank details, or Aadhaar numbers on unsecure websites.

YouTube Guide

Learn everything about YouTube - from searching videos to staying safe. Perfect for beginners and first-time users.

How to Search Videos

Open the YouTube app or website. At the top, you'll see a search bar. Type the video name or topic you want to watch and press the search button. You'll see many results - choose the video that looks correct and has good views and ratings.

YouTube search bar at the top of the page

What is a Playlist?

A playlist is a collection of videos saved together. You can create your own playlist to watch videos later. Click on the "Save" button below a video and select "Create New Playlist". Give it a name and save it.

Creating and managing playlists

Subscribe to Channels

If you like a channel, click the "Subscribe" button below the video. After subscribing, click the bell icon to receive notifications when new videos are uploaded. This way you never miss content from your favorite creators.

Subscribe button and notification bell

Comments & Interaction

Scroll down below any video to read and write comments. You can like/dislike videos, reply to comments, and share videos with friends. Remember to be respectful and never share personal information in comments.

Comments Section
Engaging with videos through comments

YouTube Shorts

YouTube Shorts are short vertical videos (15-60 seconds). You can scroll through them by swiping up. Like, comment, and share Shorts just like regular videos. They're perfect for quick entertainment and learning.

Shorts
Vertical short videos format

YouTube Studio

If you upload videos, YouTube Studio is your dashboard. Check video performance, respond to comments, manage your channel, and see analytics. Tap your profile picture and select "YouTube Studio" to access it.

Analytics Dashboard

YouTube Safety Tips

Protect Personal Info

Never share your address, phone number, or financial details in comments or live chats.

Suspicious Links

Avoid clicking on links in video descriptions or comments from unknown sources.

Restricted Mode

Enable restricted mode to filter out potentially mature content for children.

Fake Giveaways

Don't trust messages claiming you've won prizes or giveaways - they're usually scams.

Report Inappropriate Content

Use the report button to flag inappropriate videos, comments, or channels.

Watch History Privacy

Clear your watch history regularly or use incognito mode for private viewing.

Quick YouTube Tips

• Double-tap video to seek 10 seconds forward/backward
• Swipe down to minimize video and continue browsing
• Use picture-in-picture mode to watch while using other apps
• Download videos offline for watching without internet
• Create multiple playlists for different topics

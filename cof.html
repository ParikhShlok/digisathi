Common Online Frauds & Scams

Learn to identify and avoid common digital scams targeting vulnerable communities. Knowledge is your best protection against online fraudsters.

Phishing Scams

Fake emails or messages pretending to be banks or companies to steal passwords and personal details.

High Risk
Never share passwords via email

OTP & Banking Frauds

Scammers ask for OTP, PIN, or CVV to steal money from bank accounts.

High Risk
Never share OTP with anyone

Lottery & Prize Scams

Messages claiming you won a prize or lottery and asking for fees or details.

Medium Risk
Real prizes never ask for fees

Fake Shopping Websites

Fake online stores offering huge discounts but never delivering products.

Medium Risk
Check website reviews before buying

Impersonation Calls

Calls pretending to be police, banks, or government officials asking for money or information.

High Risk
Verify caller identity independently

Job & Work Scams

Fake job offers asking for registration fees or personal details.

Medium Risk
Real jobs don't ask for upfront fees

Fake App Scams

Malicious apps that steal data or money after installation.

High Risk
Only download from official stores

Online Romance Scams

Fake relationships created to emotionally manipulate victims for money.

Medium Risk
Be cautious with online relationships

Malicious Link Scams

Dangerous links sent via SMS or WhatsApp that steal login information.

High Risk
Don't click unknown links

About DigiSathi

Empowering rural India with essential digital literacy and safety education. Making technology accessible, understandable, and safe for everyone.

Our Mission & Purpose

DigiSathi is designed to spread cyber knowledge and digital safety awareness among rural communities. Our goal is to help everyone use the internet safely, avoid online frauds, and protect personal information through simple, accessible education.

Our Purpose

Many people lose money and data due to lack of cyber knowledge. DigiSathi helps prevent cyber crimes by spreading awareness in simple language with real-world examples.

Who Can Use This Platform?

Farmers, students, senior citizens, shopkeepers, and anyone who uses a mobile phone or internet in rural areas. Designed for first-time smartphone users.

What You Will Learn

  • How to create strong passwords
  • How to identify fraud messages
  • How to avoid online scams
  • How to protect personal information
  • Safe use of digital payments

Our Vision

To create a digitally safe rural India where everyone can use technology confidently without fear of cyber frauds. We believe that knowledge is the best protection against online threats.

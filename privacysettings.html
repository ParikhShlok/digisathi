Essential Security

Protect Your Online Accounts

Simple, effective steps to keep your digital accounts safe from hackers and scammers. Hover over each card to learn more about protecting yourself.

Strong Passwords

Hover to learn more

Use Strong & Unique Passwords

Create long passwords with mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse the same password across different accounts.

Two-Factor Authentication

Hover to learn more

Enable 2-Step Verification

Add an extra layer of security with SMS codes or authentication apps. Protects your account even if password is stolen.

Avoid Phishing

Hover to learn more

Watch for Fake Links

Never click suspicious links in emails or messages. Verify sender identity and check URLs carefully before entering login details.

Secure Logout

Hover to learn more

Log Out from Public Devices

Always log out after using accounts on shared computers or public Wi-Fi. Never check "Remember Me" on public devices.

Regular Updates

Hover to learn more

Keep Apps Updated

Update your apps, browser, and operating system regularly. Updates fix security vulnerabilities that hackers can exploit.

Activity Monitoring

Hover to learn more

Monitor Account Activity

Regularly check login alerts and activity history. Report any suspicious access immediately to prevent unauthorized use.

Stay Safe Online

Remember these key principles: Never share passwords, Enable 2FA, and Stay vigilant against suspicious messages. Your online safety is in your hands!

Common Phone Problems & Solutions

The Magic Restart

Is the phone acting strange, slow, or frozen?

Quick Fix:
  • Hold the Power button for 10 seconds
  • Tap on "Restart" when the menu appears
  • Wait for the phone to turn back on
Watch Video Guide

No Sound / Ringing

Can't hear calls, notifications, or music?

Quick Fix:
  • Press the Volume Up button on the side
  • Check if phone is in silent mode
  • Test with music or a call
Watch Video Guide

Phone is Talking (Talkback)

Is the phone speaking everything you touch?

Quick Fix:
  • Hold both Volume buttons (+ and -)
  • Keep holding for 3 seconds
  • Phone will vibrate and turn off Talkback
Watch Video Guide

Screen is Too Dark

Can't see the screen in sunlight or bright light?

Quick Fix:
  • Swipe down from the top of screen
  • Find the brightness slider
  • Slide it to the right to make brighter
Watch Video Guide

Memory is Full

Phone says "Storage Full" or is hanging often?

Quick Fix:
  • Open WhatsApp Settings
  • Go to "Storage and Data"
  • Delete old videos and files
Watch Video Guide

Wrong Language

Is the phone showing English or another language you don't understand?

Quick Fix:
  • Look for the Settings ⚙️ icon
  • Find the Language / 🌐 option
  • Select your preferred language
See Detailed Guide

Giant Screen Icons

Did the screen zoom in way too much?

Quick Fix:
  • Quickly tap the screen 3 times
  • With one finger, three fast taps
  • Screen will zoom back to normal

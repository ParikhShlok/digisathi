Your Digital Safety

Cyber Safety Awareness Guide

Essential knowledge to protect your personal information, online accounts, and digital identity from cyber threats and scams.

Identity Protection

Personal information like Aadhaar, PAN, and date of birth can be used for identity theft if not protected carefully.

High Importance

Fraud Prevention

Protecting your financial data helps prevent online scams, unauthorized transactions, and financial fraud.

Critical Protection

Location Privacy

Your location data can be misused to track movements. Be careful about sharing location in apps and social media.

Medium Priority

Strong Passwords

Long passwords with symbols and numbers are extremely difficult for hackers to crack compared to simple passwords.

Essential Security

Device Security

Strong passwords protect your phones, laptops, email accounts, and social media from unauthorized access.

Device Protection

Safe Digital Habits

Building good cybersecurity habits ensures long-term safety and makes you less vulnerable to evolving online threats.

Long-term Safety

Your Safety is Our Priority

Remember: Cybersecurity is not just about technology - it's about developing good habits and staying vigilant. A few simple precautions can protect you from most online threats. Stay informed, stay safe!

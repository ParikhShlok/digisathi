Essential Guide

Basic Internet Safety

Learn the fundamental practices to stay safe and smart while using the internet. Hover over each safety tip to learn more details.

Use Strong Passwords

Always use strong, unique passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse the same password across different websites or apps.

Why is this important?

Avoid Unknown Emails

Never click suspicious links in emails from unknown senders. Be cautious of emails asking for personal information or urgent action.

Why is this important?

Protect Personal Information

Never share sensitive personal details online like Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, or passwords with anyone through email, messages, or calls.

Why is this important?

Use Secure Websites

Always check for HTTPS and lock icon in the browser address bar. Never enter personal information on websites without proper security.

Why is this important?

Stay Vigilant Online

Remember: If something looks suspicious, it probably is. Trust your instincts and always verify before clicking links or sharing information. Your online safety is your responsibility.

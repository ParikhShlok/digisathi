🛡️ Avoid Scams and Frauds

Essential tips for digital safety

🤫
Never Share Your OTP

Banks never ask for OTP. Sharing it allows hackers to steal money instantly.

🚫
Don't Click Unknown Links

Links in suspicious SMS or WhatsApp messages can steal your personal data.

📞
Verify Unknown Calls

Beware of callers pretending to be officials or relatives asking for urgent money.

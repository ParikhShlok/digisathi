Avoid Unknown Emails

Essential safety checklist to protect yourself from email-based threats, phishing attempts, and malicious content in your inbox.

⚠️ Email Safety Warning

Unknown emails are one of the most common ways cybercriminals attack. Always verify the sender before opening attachments or clicking links.

  • Malware & Virus Threats

    Unknown emails may contain viruses, malware, or ransomware that can damage your device, steal data, or lock your files.

    High Risk

  • Phishing Attempts

    Many fake emails are phishing attempts designed to trick you into revealing passwords, bank details, or other personal information.

    Very High Risk

  • Financial Fraud

    Clicking links in suspicious emails can lead to financial fraud, unauthorized transactions, or theft of banking credentials.

    Critical Risk

  • Malicious Links

    Suspicious attachments or links may redirect you to unsafe websites that install harmful software or steal your data.

    High Risk

  • Identity Theft

    Hackers can use fake emails to gather enough information to steal your identity online and commit fraud in your name.

    Severe Risk

  • Urgency & Panic Tactics

    Scammers create false urgency or panic to trick users into quick, unthinking actions that compromise security.

    Medium Risk

  • Account Security

    Avoiding unknown emails helps keep all your online accounts, devices, and personal data secure from unauthorized access.

    Essential Protection

Email Safety Checklist

  • Always verify the sender's email address before opening
  • Never click links or download attachments from unknown senders
  • Look for spelling errors and poor grammar in emails
  • Check for HTTPS and lock icon on any linked websites
  • Use spam filters and email security features
  • Report suspicious emails to your email provider
  • When in doubt, delete the email without opening
